Common Questions

Does applying affect my credit score?

Most lenders use a soft credit pull for the initial pre-qualification, which doesn't affect your score. A hard pull may occur when you confirm your loan. The lender will tell you before it happens.

What credit score do I need?

Requirements vary by lender and plan. Generally, scores in the 580–620+ range can qualify for some programs. Lenders look at the full picture — income and payment history matter too.

Do I have to finance the full amount?

No. You can finance a portion and pay the rest upfront. For example, pay the deposit in cash and finance the balance after the job is complete.

Is there a minimum job size for financing?

Most lenders have a minimum of $500 – $1,000 to qualify. Smaller jobs are typically easier to pay upfront or split across two cards. Ask us and we'll tell you exactly what's available for your quote.

When do I apply?

After you get your quote and decide you want to move forward. We'll send you the financing link — fill it out, pick your plan, and we schedule the job. The whole thing usually takes less than 10 minutes.

Ready to get started?

Get your instant quote first — that's always step one. Once you have a number, we'll walk you through the financing options that fit.
