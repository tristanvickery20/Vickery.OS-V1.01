Services — Site Visit Required (No Instant Pricing)
Electrical Panel Upgrade Site Visit
Why site visit Utility coordination, main breaker sizing, meter base, and grounding system must be assessed in person before pricing. Customer told "We need to assess your current panel, meter, and utility connection before we can give you an accurate quote. We’ll schedule a free on-site assessment."
Sub-Panel Installation Site Visit
Why site visit Feeder cable run, panel location, and main panel capacity must be assessed on-site.
EV Charger Installation Site Visit
Why site visit Panel capacity check, conduit run distance, and charger model selection must be confirmed before pricing. Customer told "EV charger installs depend on your panel capacity and run distance. We’ll do a free site visit to confirm everything."
Hot Tub / Spa Circuit Site Visit
Why site visit Dedicated 240V run, GFCI disconnect location, and conduit path must be assessed on-site.
Electrical Troubleshooting / Diagnostics Site Visit
Why site visit Diagnostic and troubleshooting work is inherently variable — cannot be priced without seeing the fault. Customer told "Troubleshooting requires us to physically find the problem. We charge a diagnostic fee and provide a repair quote on-site."
Whole-House / Partial Rewiring Site Visit
Why site visit Scope, circuit count, wall access, and knob-and-tube assessment all require in-person evaluation.
Standby / Backup Generator Site Visit
Why site visit Transfer switch selection, fuel source routing, and panel assessment require on-site visit.
Commercial Work (Lighting retrofit, ballast, exit / emergency lights) Site Visit
Why site visit Fixture count, control scope, code compliance, and permitting requirements vary too widely for remote quoting.

Pricing Rules & Policies
Minimum Ticket
$150 minimum for any dispatched job. Jobs quoting below minimum are automatically flagged for review before confirmation.
Deposit Policy
No deposit required for jobs under $500. For jobs $500–$2,000: 25% deposit required to schedule. For jobs over $2,000: 50% deposit required to schedule. Deposit collected via invoice before crew is dispatched.
Change Orders
Any scope change discovered on-site must be documented as a change order before additional work proceeds. Change orders are approved in writing (text confirmation is acceptable) by the customer. No verbal-only change orders.
Diagnostic / Troubleshooting Rate
Troubleshooting jobs are dispatched as site visits. The diagnostic fee is applied toward the repair if the customer proceeds. Rate: confirm current rate in Config sheet.
Material Cost Policy
Instant quotes include a material estimate. Final invoice reflects actual material quantities used. Customers are informed of this at quote time via the material disclosure notice.
Permits
Permit fees are passed through to the customer at cost. No markup on permit fees. Permit pulled by Master Electrician of Record before work begins on permitted jobs.
Price Rounding
All customer-facing prices are rounded to the nearest $5. Internal price calculations use full precision.

All Four Tests Must Pass to Receive Bonus
40% Gross Margin: (Collected Revenue − Direct Job Cost) ÷ Collected Revenue ≥ 40%. Direct job cost includes loaded labor (base rate × burden %), materials, permits, and subcontractors.
Time & Photo Documentation: At least one time entry and one job photo must be submitted for this job by 11:59 PM on the day of service.
14-Day Quality Window: No customer complaint, callback, or warranty claim within 14 calendar days of job completion.
No Unauthorized Budget Deviation: Job must not have an unauthorized_deviation flag set in the CRM. Any scope or cost change must go through a documented change order.

Bonus Payroll Timing
Per-job bonuses are paid in the payroll period following the calendar month in which the job closed and all four tests passed. Profit-share is calculated quarterly and paid within 30 days of each quarter close.
