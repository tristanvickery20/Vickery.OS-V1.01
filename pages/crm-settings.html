Connect Personal Calendar
The Personal calendar is your own Google Calendar — the service account reads it as read-only to block out your unavailable times when suggesting job slots. Google does not allow service accounts to create or own personal calendars, so you connect it in three steps:
  1. Copy the Service Account Email shown in the status grid above.
  2. In Google Calendar → Settings → your personal calendar → Share with specific people → add that email with See only free/busy (hide details) permission.
  3. Copy your personal Calendar ID (Settings → your calendar → scroll to bottom) and paste it below, then click Test Connection.

iPhone / Google Account Setup
Share Calendars with Your Google Account
To see CRM jobs on your iPhone, share the two managed calendars with your personal Google account:
  1. Run Bootstrap Calendars above.
  2. Open Google Calendar while signed in as the service account, or ask your admin to share "Vickery Jobs" and "Vickery Internal" with your personal Google account.
  3. Accept the sharing invite — the calendars will appear on your iPhone automatically via Google Calendar app.
