Connect Personal Calendar
The Personal calendar is your own Google Calendar — the service account uses it to block your unavailable times in job scheduling and to write Personal Block events you create in the CRM. Google does not allow service accounts to create personal-type calendars, so you bring your own in three steps:
  1. Copy the Service Account Email shown in the status grid above.
  2. In Google Calendar → Settings → your personal calendar → Share with specific people → add that email with Make changes to events permission (required to create Personal Blocks).
  3. Copy your personal Calendar ID (Settings → your calendar → scroll to bottom) and paste it below, then click Test Connection.

iPhone / Google Account Setup
Share Calendars with Your Google Account
How this works: The CRM uses a Google service account (not a per-user OAuth login) to own and manage the "Vickery Jobs" and "Vickery Internal" calendars. To see those calendars on your iPhone, share them with your personal Google account:
  1. Run Bootstrap Calendars above to create/verify the managed calendars.
  2. Ask your admin to share "Vickery Jobs" and "Vickery Internal" with your personal Google account (the service account owns them, so this is done via the Google Workspace admin or service-account calendar settings).
  3. Accept the sharing invite — both calendars will appear on your iPhone via the Google Calendar app.
Note: This is a service-account-based integration. There is no per-user "Connect your Google account" OAuth flow.
