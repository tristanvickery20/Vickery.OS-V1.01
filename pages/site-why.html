Vickery Electric — Southeast Texas

We do things differently.

Transparent pricing. Southeast Texas roots. Work that holds up. Here's what we stand behind and why it matters.

Most electricians make you play phone tag for a ballpark estimate, then show up and hit you with a number that changed. We built a better model — price first, then show up. That's it. That's the whole thing.

1

Upfront Pricing — No Estimate Theater

You get a firm number before we ever knock on your door. Our online estimator calculates labor, materials, overhead, and travel together — so the price you see is the price we work from. If something genuinely changes scope during the job, we talk before we proceed.

2

Licensed, Insured, and Accountable

We are fully licensed electricians operating under the Texas rules that protect your home and your investment. We carry proper liability insurance. If something doesn't hold up, you call us — not an 800 number, not a franchise center. Us, directly.

3

Southeast Texas Roots

We're based in Orange, TX. This is our market, our neighbors, and our reputation. We're not parachuting in from Houston for a quick ticket. We're building a service business that earns repeat calls and referrals — which means we have every reason to do the job right the first time.

4

Photo-Based Quotes, Not Wild Guesses

For panel work and anything with real scope ambiguity, we ask for photos before we commit to a number. This protects both sides — you don't get a surprise, and we don't get burned on a job that needed three extra hours nobody budgeted for. Honest assessment from the start.

5

We Say No When We Should

If a job is too far, too uncertain, or below the threshold where we can do it right and still cover our costs, we'll tell you that up front rather than take the work and cut corners. A good contractor knows which jobs to take. We'd rather refer you to someone better suited than do work we can't stand behind.
