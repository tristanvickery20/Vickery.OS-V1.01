Service Agreement

How We Work Together

This is our plain-English commitment to you — what you can expect from us and what we ask of you.

Effective May 1, 2025 · Vickery Electric · Orange County, TX · TECL # 37591

Your Estimate

The price shown is our honest, good-faith estimate based on the information you provided. We make every effort to complete your job at or near that number. It is not a binding contract — materials are confirmed on-site and actual conditions sometimes differ from what's visible in photos or described online.

If the Price Needs to Change

If our technician arrives and finds that the actual scope, location, access, materials, wiring, or panel condition differs from what was described, we will stop and discuss it with you before doing any additional work. You are never surprised with a higher bill after the job is done. If you decline the revised price, you owe nothing beyond a standard trip fee for the visit.

Site Access & Safe Conditions

When you book an appointment, we ask that you:

  • Secure all pets before our tech arrives
  • Ensure the work area is safely accessible
  • Be reachable by phone during the appointment window
  • For unattended jobs — provide complete, accurate access instructions

If our technician cannot safely access or verify the work area, the appointment may be rescheduled. A trip fee applies if we travel to the job and cannot begin work due to access issues.

Payment

Payment is due upon job completion unless a deposit arrangement was agreed to in advance. We accept cash, check, and card. For larger jobs, a deposit may be required to hold your scheduled date. Financing options are available — ask us about Wisetack or see our financing page.

Scheduling & Cancellations

We hold your slot for you and turn away other jobs to accommodate it. If you need to reschedule, please give us at least 24 hours notice. Last-minute cancellations (under 2 hours before arrival) may be subject to a trip fee. We'll always do our best to accommodate changes — just call or text us.

Our Work & Warranty

All labor performed by Vickery Electric is warranted against defects for 1 year from the date of service. If something we installed or repaired fails within that period due to our workmanship, we'll fix it at no charge. Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, subsequent modifications, or acts of nature.

Questions? This document is meant to be informational — we want you to feel confident and informed before we ever set foot on your property. If you have any questions, call or text us at (409) 813-9525 or email info@vickeryelectric.com.
