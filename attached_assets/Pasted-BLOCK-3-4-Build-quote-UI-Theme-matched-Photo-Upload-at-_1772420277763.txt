BLOCK 3/4 — Build /quote UI (Theme-matched) + Photo Upload at Lock-In

Goal: Create a customer-facing /quote page that matches your existing dark theme, follows V3 flow, and calls the APIs from blocks 1–2. Scheduling comes in block 4.

Constraints:
	•	Reuse pages/css/brand.css and existing header/nav styles.
	•	Keep UI minimal, fast, mobile-first.
	•	Price must show before lead info.
	•	No Good/Better/Best.

Work to do:
	1.	Add route:

	•	GET /quote should serve pages/quote.html (public).

	2.	Create pages/quote.html

	•	Use your existing header shell and nav button styles.
	•	Steps layout:

Step A: Service
	•	Show clickable service tiles from /api/quote/config (JobTypes where active=true).

Step B: Questions (3–5) + Add-ons
	•	Render only questions tied to that job_type_id.
	•	Answers as simple homeowner language.
	•	Add-ons as toggles/checkboxes.

Step C: Show Price (before lead info)

	•	call /api/quote/calc
	•	Display:
	•	“Your Instant Price: $X”
	•	Small friendly line: “Exact price based on your answers.”
	•	Under it show the travel disclaimer:
	•	“A $25 travel fee may be applied once address is confirmed.”
	•	BNPL block (text only for now): “As low as $__/mo with flexible payment options.”

Step D: Lock My Price & Schedule
	•	Button opens lead form:
	•	name, phone, email
	•	address + zip (zip required if you can’t parse it reliably)
	•	On submit call /api/quote/lock
	•	Update price live after zip entered (re-run lock call or a lightweight “reprice” call)

	•	“Next: Choose a time” (scheduling in block 4)

Acceptance checks:
