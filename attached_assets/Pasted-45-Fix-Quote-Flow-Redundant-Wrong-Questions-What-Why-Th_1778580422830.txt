45 - Fix Quote Flow — Redundant & Wrong Questions

What & Why

The quote flow asks customers questions whose answers are already known from earlier steps. The most glaring example: SERVICE_QUANTITY ("How many items need this work?") is asked in Step 4 (Questions) even though the customer already set a quantity with the +/− control in Step 3 (Services). PROPERTY_TYPE ("Residential or Commercial?") is asked mid-flow even though the customer chose Residential or Commercial on the very first screen.

Additionally, PERMIT_NEEDED appears across many service maps but has zero pricing effect (all options → multiplier 1.0) and is not a question customers can meaningfully answer — it's a scheduling/admin concern. And a handful of services have question ordering that buries the most impactful question below noise.

Done looks like

Completing the quote flow for any service never asks for information already given in a prior step
SERVICE_QUANTITY question no longer appears for any service — the qty from Step 3 silently drives the same volume-discount multiplier as before
PROPERTY_TYPE question no longer appears for any service — segment from Step 1 silently drives the same 1.15× commercial multiplier as before
PERMIT_NEEDED is removed from all consumer-facing service question maps (it appears on no service with a pricing impact)
Per-service question lists are reordered so the highest-impact question (usually REPLACE_OR_NEW or the main scope fork) comes first
Pricing results are identical before and after — the only change is the customer is no longer asked questions already answered
Pricing change disclosure

No net pricing change. Both suppressed modules have their answers auto-injected by the client before the calc call:

Suppressed module	Auto-injected value	How it's derived
SERVICE_QUANTITY	"1" / "2_4" / "5_8" / "9plus"	Mapped from svc.qty: 1→"1", 2–4→"2_4", 5–8→"5_8", ≥9→"9plus"
PROPERTY_TYPE	"residential" / "commercial"	Mapped from S.segment.toLowerCase()
Multipliers remain exactly as defined in the seed: SERVICE_QUANTITY gives 0.9/0.85/0.8 for volume. PROPERTY_TYPE gives 1.15 for commercial. Neither changes.

PERMIT_NEEDED has multiplier 1.0 for all options in every service map — removing it from the question list has zero effect on any price output.

Out of scope

Changing any multiplier values in the engine or seed data
Adding new questions to services not already in scope
Rewiring how qty flows through the V2 engine (it already receives qty from selectedServices[i].qty)
Steps

Auto-inject already-known answers before calc/lock — In quote.js, just before building the answers payload for /api/quote/calc and /api/quote/lock, inject SERVICE_QUANTITY mapped from the service's qty, and PROPERTY_TYPE mapped from S.segment. These are only injected if not already in S.answers (so manual overrides are preserved). Add a helper autoInjectKnownAnswers() called from both calcPrice() and submitLock().
Suppress known-answer modules from the visible question list — In enrichConfigWithModules() (or directly in renderQuestions()), filter out SERVICE_QUANTITY and PROPERTY_TYPE from the questions array for every service type, since they are always auto-injected. Also filter out PERMIT_NEEDED from all service maps since it has no pricing impact.
Update seed data modules_csv — In lib/estimatorSeedData.js, remove SERVICE_QUANTITY, PROPERTY_TYPE, and PERMIT_NEEDED from every service's modules_csv where they currently appear. This keeps the sheet in sync after next re-seed. Services affected: RECESSED_LIGHTING, LED_RETROFIT, CEILING_FAN_INSTALL, OUTLET_INSTALL, GFCI_OUTLET, DIMMER_SWITCH, SMART_SWITCH, SMOKE_CO_DETECTOR, REWIRE_WHOLE_HOUSE, REWIRE_COMMERCIAL, PANEL_UPGRADE, BREAKER_PANEL_REPAIR, SURGE_PROTECTOR, EV_CHARGER, DEDICATED_CIRCUIT, APPLIANCE_CIRCUIT, HOT_TUB_CIRCUIT, GENERATOR_STANDBY, GENERATOR_TRANSFER_SWITCH, GENERATOR_BACKUP, CODE_COMPLIANCE, FIRE_ALARM, TRANSFORMER_INSTALL, COMM_LIGHTING_RETROFIT, BALLAST_REPLACE, EXIT_EMERGENCY_LIGHTS, TROUBLESHOOT_FLICKER, BREAKER_TRIPPING.
Fix question ordering per service — Reorder modules_csv for each instant/instant-with-safeguards service so the primary price-fork question comes first (REPLACE_OR_NEW, CIRCUIT_SCOPE, SMOKE_TYPE, EXISTING_WIRING, FAN_RATED_BOX, etc.), followed by access/location modifiers (CEILING_HEIGHT, ATTIC_ACCESS, WALL_TYPE), then soft signals (HOME_AGE, ROOM_LOCATION), and photo + UNCERTAINTY_BUFFER last. Services requiring reorder: RECESSED_LIGHTING, LIGHT_FIXTURE_INSTALL, CEILING_FAN_INSTALL, OUTLET_INSTALL, GFCI_OUTLET, DIMMER_SWITCH, SMART_SWITCH, OUTDOOR_LIGHTING, MOTION_SECURITY_LIGHT.
Force sheet re-seed — After updating estimatorSeedData.js, bump the stale-seed detection token in lib/estimatorModulesConfig.js so the corrected question maps are written to the Estimator sheet on next server start without any manual intervention.
Verify no regressions — After all changes, manually step through the quote flow for: Ceiling Fan, Recessed Lighting, Outlet Install, GFCI, Dimmer Switch, Smart Switch, EV Charger, Smoke Detector, Panel Upgrade, Outdoor Lighting. Confirm SERVICE_QUANTITY and PROPERTY_TYPE no longer appear, prices are unchanged from a fresh flow with the same inputs, and question count per service is ≤ 8 visible questions.
