VICKERY ELECTRIC PAYMENT + FINANCING + INTERNAL BOOKS STACK GOAL

Update the Vickery Electric CRM / instant quote / booking / job closeout system around the correct payment and financing stack.

Important correction:
Do NOT assume QuickBooks is part of the stack.
Do NOT build around QuickBooks.
Do NOT make QuickBooks the accounting source of truth.

Vickery Electric wants most books, payment tracking, job financials, deposits, fees, balances, cash records, financing status, payouts, and job-level profitability tracked inside the CRM itself.

This is not a tiny label change. Treat this as a serious payment, financing, and internal bookkeeping system build.

The CRM is the business operating system.

CORE STACK

1. Square = primary regular payment processor

Square should be the main payment tool for normal payments.

Square handles:
- In-person card payments
- Tap to Pay
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Card payments
- Payment links
- Invoice-style payment requests
- Deposits
- Final balances
- Remote payments
- Cash recording support
- Small-ticket pay-later through Afterpay if eligible

2. Square Afterpay = small-ticket pay-later

Afterpay should be treated as the small-ticket pay-later option.

Use it for:
- Tickets starting around $100
- Small service jobs
- Tickets under $500
- Some medium jobs where eligible

Customer-facing language:
- “Pay Later”
- “Split payments if eligible”
- “Pay over time if eligible”

Do NOT call this true contractor financing.

3. Wisetack = larger home-service financing

Wisetack should be treated as the main larger-ticket financing provider.

Use it for:
- Jobs $500+
- Medium electrical jobs
- Large electrical jobs
- Panel-related work
- Generator-related work
- Bundled service packages
- Projects up to at least $15,000

Customer-facing language:
- “Monthly payment options”
- “Check financing options”
- “See if this project qualifies for monthly payments”

4. CRM = internal books / financial command center

The CRM should track the business financial picture directly.

The CRM needs to track:
- Quotes
- Deposits
- Final balances
- Payments received
- Payment method
- Processing fees
- Financing fees
- Cash payments
- Refunds
- Failed payments
- Balance due
- Labor cost
- Material cost
- Expense cost
- Job gross profit
- Job gross margin
- Daily revenue
- Weekly revenue
- Monthly revenue
- Accounts receivable
- Unpaid jobs
- Partially paid jobs
- Paid jobs
- Collected vs uncollected money
- Financing status
- Net payout after fees
- Owner dashboard numbers

There should be no QuickBooks dependency for this build.

MAIN BUSINESS GOAL

Vickery Electric needs an easy customer payment experience and a strong internal financial tracking system.

Customers should be able to pay in person or virtually.

Customers should be able to use:
- Cash
- Card
- Tap to Pay
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Payment link
- ACH / bank transfer if Square supports it
- Pay Later for small tickets
- Monthly financing for larger tickets
- Deposit + final balance
- Remote payment from spouse / landlord / property manager

The CRM should clearly know:
- What the customer owes
- What has been paid
- How it was paid
- What fees were taken
- What net amount Vickery Electric actually receives
- Whether the job is allowed to schedule
- Whether the job is allowed to close
- Whether the job is financially complete

CUSTOMER-FACING PAYMENT LOGIC

Under $100:
- Show normal payment options only
- Cash
- Card
- Tap to Pay
- Digital wallet
- Payment link
- Do not emphasize pay-later
- Do not show Wisetack

$100 to $499:
- Show normal payment options
- Show Pay Later / Afterpay if available
- Do not prioritize Wisetack
- Label as “Pay later if eligible”
- This is a convenience option, not formal financing

$500 to $2,000:
- Show normal payment options
- Show Pay Later if available
- Show Wisetack monthly payment options
- Customer can choose:
  - Pay now
  - Pay deposit
  - Pay at completion if allowed
  - Payment link
  - Pay Later
  - Monthly financing

$2,000 to $15,000:
- Prioritize Wisetack / monthly payment options
- Still allow card, ACH, deposit, and payment link
- Larger jobs may require manual review before full booking
- Financing accepted or deposit paid can satisfy scheduling/payment gate depending on settings

High-risk jobs:
- If the quote has risk flags, photo gates, generator scope, panel scope, attic uncertainty, unknown wiring conditions, or large labor uncertainty, the system should be able to block auto-booking until review/payment requirements are met

CUSTOMER-FACING PAYMENT LANGUAGE

Use clean wording like:

“Flexible payment options available.”

“Pay by cash, card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, payment link, or approved pay-later options.”

“Small-ticket pay-later may be available on eligible jobs.”

“For qualifying larger projects, monthly payment options may be available.”

Avoid:
- Guaranteed approval
- Everyone qualifies
- No credit needed
- We finance everyone
- 0% financing
- Only $X/month unless provided directly by the provider
- Card surcharge language
- Any lending promises Vickery Electric cannot legally make

ONLINE QUOTE / BOOKING GOAL

The instant quote flow should show payment options based on the quote total and risk level.

Customer should be able to:
- See price
- See payment options
- Pay now
- Pay deposit
- Request/send payment link
- Choose pay-later if eligible
- Check monthly payment options if eligible
- Upload photos if required
- Book only when payment/financing/review rules allow it

Important:
Do not allow “financing link sent” to count as paid.
Do not allow “customer started financing” to count as paid.
Financing accepted/funded can count as satisfying the payment requirement, depending on provider status.

BOOKING GATE GOAL

The system should eventually support rules like:

- Small jobs can be booked with pay-at-completion if allowed
- Jobs requiring deposit cannot move to Scheduled until deposit is paid or financing is accepted
- Large/high-risk jobs can require manual review before scheduling
- Financing link sent does not equal approved
- Financing approved does not equal accepted
- Financing accepted/funded can satisfy the payment requirement
- Cash jobs need collection/handoff/verification tracking
- Jobs cannot be financially closed with unresolved balance
- Jobs cannot be financially closed with unverified cash
- Jobs cannot be financially closed without job costs entered unless admin override is used

WHAT NOT TO DO

Do not build fake lending logic.

Do not calculate APR.

Do not promise approval.

Do not make Vickery Electric responsible for underwriting.

Do not add card surcharge language.

Do not assume QuickBooks exists.

Do not build around QuickBooks.

Do not remove the CRM’s internal financial tracking direction.

Do not make payment status and financing status the same thing.

Do not count a sent financing link as payment.

Do not count started financing as payment.

Do not clutter the customer quote page.

Do not make the tech explain financing terms.

Do not delete or rewrite existing working quote/booking/job logic unless necessary.

SUCCESS CRITERIA

When finished, the system should clearly reflect this business goal:

Vickery Electric uses Square for regular in-person and virtual payments, Square Afterpay for small-ticket pay-later starting around $100, Wisetack for larger home-service financing starting around $500, and the Vickery Electric CRM itself as the financial command center and internal books system.

The CRM should understand payment status, financing status, pay-later status, deposits, balances, cash, fees, payouts, job profitability, and financial closeout without depending on QuickBooks.
