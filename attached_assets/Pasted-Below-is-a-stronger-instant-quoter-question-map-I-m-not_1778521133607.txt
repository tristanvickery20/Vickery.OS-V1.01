Below is a stronger instant-quoter question map. I’m not limiting it to your current allowed rows. This is the version I’d want the sheet to support.

Rule of thumb:

Every question should do at least one of these:

1. Add labor
2. Add material
3. Add travel/access difficulty
4. Add risk buffer
5. Require photo
6. Require manual review
7. Disqualify instant quote / force site visit

⸻

Universal Questions Every Instant Quote Should Capture

These should appear on almost every service, either visibly or behind the scenes.

Code	Customer Question	Why It Matters
PROPERTY_TYPE	What type of property is this?	House, mobile home, apartment, condo, commercial all change rules
HOME_AGE	About how old is the home?	Older homes raise wiring, grounding, box, and panel risk
CEILING_HT	How high is the work area?	Ladder time, safety, two-man work
ACCESS_DIFFICULTY	Is the area easy to reach?	Furniture, attic, tight spaces, outside height
EXISTING_WIRING	Is there already power/wiring at the location?	Replacement vs new install
WALL_CEILING_TYPE	What surface are we working in?	Drywall, brick, siding, plaster, tile, wood
PANEL_DISTANCE	About how far is the work area from the main panel?	Circuit labor/material
PANEL_CONDITION	Does your panel look modern and labeled?	Breaker compatibility and safety
PHOTO_GATE	Can you upload a photo of the area?	Replaces site visit
CUSTOMER_UNSURE	Are you unsure about any answer?	Adds buffer/review flag

Best Customer-Facing Wording

Don’t make it feel like an interrogation. Use this framing:

“We ask a few job-specific questions so we can price this like we already walked the job. If you’re not sure, choose ‘Not sure’ and we’ll review your photos before confirming.”

For risky jobs:

“Based on your answers, this job needs review before final booking. You can still submit photos now so we can confirm pricing without making you schedule a full site visit.”

For price confidence:

“Your price is based on the assumptions shown below. If the photos match your answers, we’ll do our best to honor this price.”

⸻

Important Opinion

Your current system is under-questioned.

For an instant quoter, 2 questions per service is not enough unless the service is extremely simple. It will either:

1. Underprice risky work,
2. Overprice easy work,
3. Force too many site visits,
4. Or create customer anger when the tech arrives and the job is different.

You need 7–12 questions per service, but the customer should not see all of them at once. Use conditional logic.

Example:

For ceiling fan:

1. Existing fan or new location?
2. If existing fan: ask fan-rated box/photo.
3. If new location: ask attic access, switch control, ceiling type.
4. If outdoor: ask damp rating.
5. If high ceiling: review flag.

That makes the quote feel clean while still protecting you.
